# Photo library (per vertical)

Used for the 90-day side of the visuals when a business has fewer than 3 photos of its own:
the hero shows what its profile looks like after our photo session. After signup, the owner's
real photos replace these.

- One folder per vertical: `cafe/`, `restaurant/`, `auto_repair/`, `salon_barber/`, `default/`.
- 4-8 images each, named so they sort in display order (`1-hero.jpg`, `2-latest.jpg`, ...).
- Use photos you own or that are licensed for commercial use (for example Unsplash or Pexels licenses, or your own shoots).

`cafe/` is seeded with the CC0 demo images from scikit-image and a rendered menu board.
