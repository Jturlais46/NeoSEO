## 2. Ideal customer profile

**Company:**
- independent, 1-3 locations;
- owner-operated;
- walk-in or appointment customers;
- in a "near me" category;
- average ticket of $40 or more, or a high lifetime value.

**Profile signals** (from lead data):
- **Established:** operating, 15-150 reviews, rating 4.0 or higher.
- **Clear gap:** review count below the median of the 3 businesses shown next to them; few owner replies (under 30%); fewer than 20 photos, or none recent; a generic primary category or missing secondary categories; no updates in 90+ days.

**Person:**
- the owner or general manager;
- time-poor;
- skeptical of "SEO" calls;
- values being in control.

### Segments and entry offers

| Segment | Share of outreach | Hook | Entry offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Neglected** (claimed, weak) | ~60% | "Here's your profile, fixed" | The audit page, then Keep |
| **Behind on reviews** (good profile, few recent reviews) | ~25% | "You have 38 reviews; the 3 places next to you average 148" | Review kit plus Keep |
| **Unclaimed** | ~15% | "Anyone can edit your listing right now" | Free claim-and-verify help, then Keep |

### Verticals to pilot (pick 2)

| Vertical | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **Auto repair and tires** | High ticket, trust-driven, walk-in counter (the kit fits), many neglected profiles | Some shops aren't very online |
| **Salons, barbers, nails** | Repeat visits, a counter, owners care about image | Booking platforms (Fresha, Booksy) already ask for reviews |
| **Independent restaurants and cafés** | Huge "near me" demand; photos and menus matter | Thin margins, many competing agencies |
| Home services (plumbers, HVAC) | Highest ticket | No counter, owners' mobile numbers, a spam-heavy and suspension-heavy category |
| Dental and medical | Big budgets | HIPAA risk in review replies; well served by existing agencies |

**Recommendation:** auto repair plus salons/barbers. Both have a counter, repeat trust dynamics and neglected profiles. After 8 weeks, keep the vertical that converts better, and write vertical-specific pages and copy for it.

## 3. Positioning

> **For** independent local businesses that know their Google profile matters but never get around to it,
> **Mapkeeper is** the monthly caretaker for your Google Business Profile.
> **Unlike** DIY tools you won't open, or agencies that lock you in,
> **we show you the fixed profile before you pay anything**, keep it complete and collecting reviews every month, and you always stay the owner.

**Tagline options:**
- "Your Google profile, kept." (recommended)
- "Found. Chosen. Kept."
- "The upkeep your Google profile never gets."

### Messaging pillars

1. **See it before you pay.** Every prospect gets their own fixed profile: categories, description, services, photo plan and a sample review reply. The "yes" is a click.
2. **Kept, not "optimized once".** Monthly updates, photos, replies and new reviews. Profiles decay; ours don't.
3. **You stay the owner.** We're added as a manager and can be removed in one click. No contracts, no hostage-taking. Your review kit keeps working even if you leave.
4. **Honest numbers.** Monthly calls, direction requests and website clicks, taken from Google's own Performance data. No vanity "impressions" reports and no ranking guarantees.
5. **Ready for AI search.** Google's AI answers now draw on your profile, reviews and website. A complete profile is how you get quoted.

### Designed to earn trust (in a market full of scams)

| Scam pattern owners know | What we do |
|---|---|
| Robocalls "from Google" | We never cold call with a robot. Our AI assistant only calls people who asked, and says it's an AI in the first sentence. |
| "Your listing will be closed" threats | No urgency tricks, no fear copy |
| Hidden prices and contracts | Public pricing, month to month, cancel in the client portal |
| Agency takes ownership | The client stays Primary Owner; we're Manager |
| Anonymous sender | A real founder name, address and phone number on every page and email |
| Fake reviews | Written policy: no fake, bought, filtered or incentivized reviews, ever |
| "Guaranteed #1" | We say plainly that nobody can guarantee rankings |

A **"Not affiliated with Google"** line appears in every footer and on every visual.

## 4. Name

**Constraints:**
- easy to say and spell on a phone call (the voice agent will say it);
- works in English and French;
- no "Google" or "GMB" in the name (trademark);
- avoids the word "SEO", which small-business owners associate with spam calls.

**Why not NeoSEO as the customer-facing brand:**
- "SEO" triggers the scam association;
- "Neo" is generic;
- `neoseo.com`, `.io`, `.fr` and `.ai` are all registered.

NeoSEO can stay the name of the repo or holding company.

**Shortlist.** DNS check on 2026-09-26. "Free?" means no nameservers were found, which suggests the domain is available but isn't proof. **Trademark checks were not possible** in this session.

|---|---|---|---|
| **Mapkeeper** (recommended) | We keep your map listing; "keeper" implies ongoing care, which fits a subscription | Registered (possibly for sale) | mapkeeper.co, .io, .fr, .ai; getmapkeeper.com; mapkeeperhq.com |

| Pinready | Profile ready | Registered | .co, .io, .fr |

## 5. Voice and visual identity

**Voice:**
- plain, warm and specific;
- numbers over adjectives ("38 reviews vs 148", not "boost your visibility");
- no jargon ("map pack", "citations", "NAP" never reach clients);
- no exclamation marks in sales copy.

**Visual identity:**
- **Palette:** forest green `#1D6B51`, deep green `#0F4735`, amber `#D9912B` (used for stars and highlights), warm paper `#F6F2EA`, terracotta `#A8472A` (used only for "today" problems). Deliberately far from Google's blue, red, yellow and green.
- **Type:** Fraunces for headlines (warm, local, not techy) and Inter for text.
- **Logo:** a simple map pin with a "keep" ring. It's already used as a placeholder in the renderer.

## 6. Proof, to build from week 1

- **Before/after:** screenshots of our own first clients' profiles and Performance data, with their permission.
- **Case notes:** 3-4 lines each, using real numbers ("+31 reviews in 90 days, calls up 22%").
- **Named founder:** a short video of you (60 seconds, recorded once) on the homepage and in the reply that sends the audit. This does more for trust than any AI-generated asset.
